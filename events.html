Annual events & festivals

Join the celebration — from royal regattas to Hari Raya festivities.

Open house throughout the country — experience Brunei's warm hospitality and festive dishes.

Sultan's Birthday

Grand parade, fireworks and nationwide celebrations honouring His Majesty.

Showcasing local and international cinema at the Empire Cinema.

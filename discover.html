Discover Brunei

Immerse yourself in the rich culture, heritage, and flavours of the Abode of Peace.

Culture & Heritage

Brunei is a Malay Islamic Monarchy. Visitors should dress modestly (covering shoulders and knees) when visiting mosques or government buildings. Remove shoes before entering homes or places of worship. When greeting someone, it is polite to use your right hand, and public displays of affection should be avoided.

Local Cuisine & Dining

A must-try is Ambuyat, a sticky sago starch dish eaten with a bamboo fork and dipped in a sour, spicy sauce. Other favourites include Nasi Katok (rice with fried chicken and sambal) and grilled fish at the Gadong Night Market.

Overview & Location

Brunei Darussalam is a small, peaceful nation located on the northern coast of the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. It is surrounded by Malaysia and the South China Sea, offering a perfect blend of modern luxury and ancient rainforest.

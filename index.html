Why visit Brunei?

Rainforest

Over 70% of Brunei is covered in primary rainforest — home to proboscis monkeys, hornbills and rare orchids.

Islamic Heritage

Marvel at the opulent Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque and the rich Malay Islamic Monarchy culture.

Water Village

Kampong Ayer, the world's largest water village, offers a glimpse into traditional Bruneian life.

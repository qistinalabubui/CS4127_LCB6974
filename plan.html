Getting There & Around

Brunei International Airport (BWN) is the main gateway, served by Royal Brunei Airlines and other carriers. Getting around is easy with taxis, ride-hailing apps (Dart), and rental cars. Water taxis are essential for exploring Kampong Ayer.

Entry & Visa Guidance

Many nationalities (including ASEAN, UK, and US) can enter visa-free for up to 90 days. All visitors must have a passport valid for at least 6 months and a confirmed return ticket. Check the official Immigration website in our footer for specific requirements.

Best Time to Visit

Brunei has a tropical climate. The best time to visit is during the dry season from January to April. Avoid the heaviest rains which usually occur between November and December.
